FIND WHAT BELONGS TOGETHER

Find the common thread.

Select four words with something in common, then tap Submit. Find all four groups to win. Categories may be everyday things, double meanings, or words that complete a phrase.

Each puzzle has four difficulty levels: 1 Straightforward, 2 Tricky, 3 Hard, and 4 Wordplay. The final group may involve sounds, spelling, hidden words or a change to the words. Some words suggest more than one connection; look for a solution that uses all sixteen.

You have four mistakes. “One away — 3 of 4 belong together” means just one selected word needs replacing. A repeated incorrect selection doesn’t cost another mistake. Shuffle changes only the arrangement.

120 puzzles are ready to play. Finish the available collection and tap Next pack to automatically add 12 new combinations, even offline. Packs use a written category library: individual groups may recur, but complete puzzle combinations don’t repeat. New categories arrive through app updates.

When you run out of mistakes, the remaining groups are revealed. Your progress, wins and consecutive-win streak are saved on this device. Breaks and switching puzzles don’t reset a streak; losing does.

FOLLOW YOUR CURIOSITY

Find the hidden trails.

The clue links all the theme words in a 6 × 8 letter board. Connect neighbouring letters horizontally, vertically or diagonally. Tap letters, then Submit, or drag along a word and release. Tap an earlier letter to undo back to that point. Clear starts a fresh path.

Blue paths ✓ mark theme words. The gold spanning word ★ describes the whole theme and touches two opposite edges. Find all the words to use every letter exactly once.

Other dictionary words of four or more letters earn hint credit. Three different bonus words unlock one hint, which outlines the letters of an unfound theme word. Find that word before using another hint. No timer, no limit on mistakes.

Progress is saved on this device. Start with 120 original boards. When you finish, Next pack builds 12 more on your device, even offline. It may take a little time. Themes recur with new layouts and word selections; no paid service is used. These are not NYT puzzles.

A LITTLE CROSSWORD TIME

Across and down.

Fill the 8×8 grid from the clues. Every letter crosses another answer. Tap a square to select it; tap it again, or tap the clue, to switch Across and Down. Use the arrows beside the clue to move through entries, or open Clues for both lists.

Type with the on-screen or physical keyboard. Arrow keys move around the grid; Space switches direction. Tab / Shift+Tab move between clues when the grid is focused. Backspace erases a letter. Pencil marks tentative entries.

Check can mark incorrect filled tiles in the current tile, word or puzzle. Incorrect tiles get a red slash. Reveal can fill a tile, word or the whole puzzle. Revealed letters have a blue dot. Checking or revealing marks the solve as assisted.

The timer pauses when you leave this game, hide the app, open a dialog or tap Pause. Your letters and elapsed time stay on this device. 120 varied 8×8 grids are included. The earlier 7×7 collection and your saved progress remain available through the collection picker. These are independent puzzles, not NYT content.
